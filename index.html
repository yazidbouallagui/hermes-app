Hermes

Hermes is a private, single-user assistant operated by Yazid Bouallagui. It is not offered to the public.

With the owner's permission it:

  • reads the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar to send him summaries and reminders;
  • uploads and manages videos on the owner's own YouTube channels (Weird Bygones, The Why Atom) and reads their statistics.
